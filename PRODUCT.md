# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, internship coordinators, junior engineering hiring teams, university evaluators, and developers considering collaboration. They need to understand Chrislyr's identity, real software work, technical range, and software-quality mindset quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present Chrislyr John P. Tan as a serious Computer Science student who builds practical mobile, web, and scheduling software. Success means a visitor can identify his three featured projects, education, full-stack capabilities, testing discipline, traceable Jira/GitHub workflow, and contact path within a short visit without encountering inflated claims.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, and quietly experimental. The site should communicate technical confidence and care without presenting student work as senior industry experience.

## Anti-references

Generic student templates, Bootstrap-style card grids, cyberpunk motifs, neon glows, decorative code rain, fake proficiency meters, excessive glassmorphism, fabricated statistics, agency positioning, and the Sawad template's promotional badges or placeholder claims.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with shipped work, not unsupported claims.
2. Use typography and structure as the primary visual material.
3. Make software quality and testing visible alongside implementation skills.
4. Keep every statement traceable to the supplied CV or public project repositories.
5. Add motion only when it clarifies hierarchy or interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable interactions, visible focus states, WCAG-friendly contrast, meaningful alternatives, touch-safe controls, and complete support for reduced-motion preferences.
